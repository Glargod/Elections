# PolicyMatch is a recommendation, not the ballot

TrustVote 3000 splits Dynamic PolicyMatch into two stages. The questionnaire does **not** cast the vote.

## 1. Recommendation
Answers produce a ranked alignment:
- party
- score
- PlatformEval summary
- valence packet (delivery record, coalition partners, character/competence items)

That ranking is a suggestion.

## 2. Confirmation
The voter sees the person attached to that alignment and either:
- confirms,
- switches, or
- answers a few extra valence questions.

**Only the confirmed choice becomes the ballot.**

## Wording that should appear in the paper

- Purpose: Recommends a ranked policy alignment so voters can confirm (or change) a ballot that matches their priorities. PolicyMatch does not cast the vote.
- Outcome: After confirmation, the ballot reflects policy resonance. Example: “Your answers pointed to PPC; you then confirmed or changed that choice.”
- Voter journey: Jane gets the ranked alignment, sees the person attached to it, then confirms, switches, or adds valence questions. Only her confirmed choice is recorded.
- Principle: Policy over personality means policy-first recommendation, then a visible candidate and an explicit confirm step. It is not a blind assignment and not personality-driven manipulation.
